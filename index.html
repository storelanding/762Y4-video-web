Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Video app.

What can I make with Video?

Short AI videos from text prompts (Text to Video), animated clips from your photos (Image to Video), and AI images from words (Text to Art).

Which devices does it run on?

Video is an Android app, available on Google Play.

Which styles are available?

Preset styles include anime, 3D, cinematic, cartoon, realistic, fantasy and more, along with ready-made templates grouped by category.

Do I need editing experience?

No. Generation is one tap: type a prompt, add an image or pick a template, and the app creates the result for you.

Where are my videos stored?

Your creations appear in the Library under Recent, Saved and Favorite. Save a video to keep it — the app shows a notice that unsaved videos in Recent are removed after 24 hours.

Is there a subscription?

The app offers an optional subscription purchased through Google Play. You can manage or cancel it anytime in your Google Play subscription settings. See our Terms of Use for details.
